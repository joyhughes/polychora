The ray tracer draws the slice straight from the shapes, with no meshes, so shapes combine and move in real time. Give a shape its own Spin under Placement and colour to turn it through the others. Clifford tori and Hopf fibres become thin solid tubes, so they can cut too. Edges are drawn on convex polytopes.

4D → 3D projection

Stereographic projection maps the 3-sphere into 3D space from the pole at w = +1. Circles stay circles.

Centre of rotation

Spins and 4D drags turn about this point, in the object's own coordinates. Off-centre, the object swings around it, in and out of the slice and toward or away from the 4D eye.

3D camera

Drag to orbit, scroll or pinch to zoom. Shift-drag (or right-drag) turns the object in 4D. Space pause, [ ] change object, S slice.

Capture

Snapshots save as PNG and video as MP4, framed exactly as the scene is drawn. P snapshot, R start or stop recording.
